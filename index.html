Important notice

Our office closes October 30, 2026

After almost 30 years in practice, The Retina Center will be closing our doors as of 30 October 2026. Thank you for trusting us with your retinal problems over the years, we will miss caring for our patients. To help with transitions of care, a list of other retina offices is provided below. You can also look for retina resources at our professional organization’s website at ASRS.org.

Your medical records will be available for ten years from your last visit at our office. Please watch this space for updates on how to retrieve your records in the future.

We thank you for your understanding and are confident you will be able to find good retina care with one of the excellent providers on the next page.

What to do next

  1. Choose a new retina provider

    We have put together a list of retina practices in Colorado and Utah that we are confident will take good care of you, organized by region. Please contact the office of your choice directly to arrange care.
